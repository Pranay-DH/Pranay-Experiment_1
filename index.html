Field Journal Entry: The Botanical Discovery of *Sarracenia purpurea* (1842)

"14th of August, 1842 — Rain fell heavily overnight across the boglands. At first light, our expedition team ventured into the eastern marshland. It was here, beneath the shaded pine canopy, that we observed several specimens of the carnivorous Pitcher Plant."

"The leaf structure retains water efficiently, attracting indigenous insects into its interior hood. I have preserved three complete specimens in our alcohol vials for transportation back to the Royal Society collection."

[Signed] Prof. Thaddeus Sterling, Chair of Natural Philosophy.

V. Archivist's Statement & Digitization Ethics

"Preservation is not merely the static storage of physical artifacts; it is the active curation of human memory. Our mandate requires adhering strictly to non-destructive high-resolution capture techniques, maintaining provenance records, and ensuring equitable public access."

ACID-FREE STANDARDS All original physical papers are maintained at 18°C and 45% relative humidity in archival-grade boxes.
OPEN REPOSITORY ACCESS Public domain materials are digitized in lossy & lossless TIFF formats for global scholarly research.
